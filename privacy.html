This policy explains which personal data Vectory Digital collects through this website and by email, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short, because we collect very little.

This website is run by EDJ Holding B.V., trading as Vectory Digital, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 50300806, Parklaan 13, 3941 RD Doorn, the Netherlands. We are the controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at eelco@vectorydigital.nl.

What we collect, and why

Email. When you email us, we receive your name, email address, company and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to answer your request and, if you ask for it, to prepare a proposal. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Website visits. Our hosting provider records basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. We do not use this data ourselves.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes your data for us

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., based in the United States. GitHub processes visit data under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Our email is handled by Proton AG, based in Switzerland.

This policy covers the website only. Customer data in the Vectory platform is governed by the agreement with each customer.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to follow up, and at most 24 months after our last contact, unless they lead to a customer relationship.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking, and it loads no content from third parties.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Send your request to eelco@vectorydigital.nl; we respond within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
