THE CHALLENGE

Validation is a continuous effort

Getting a system validated is one milestone. Keeping it validated, through every release and every audit, is the real work.

  • Every vendor release needs a change impact assessment.
  • Periodic reviews and vendor assessments confirm that systems remain fit for intended use.
  • CSV procedures must follow changes in regulation and in your own standards.
  • Findings need follow-up through to closure.
  • Customer audits require a current and complete validation file.

A requirement — not your strategic focus

Your pharma customers expect you to be GDP compliant, and that includes computer system validation.

But CSV is a specialist field that asks for a steady, fairly limited effort. IT and Quality are busy with other priorities, and the specific expertise is often not in-house.

We close that gap, with minimal effort from your own people.

We keep you fully compliant and audit-ready for computer system validation at all times — for one monthly fee, with minimal effort from your Quality and IT teams.

HOW WE WORK

You decide. We do the work.

Under EU GDP §1.3 and Chapter 7 you remain responsible for any activity you contract out. So the split is set out in a written agreement — an auditor sees at once who does what.

  • Prepares CSV documents
  • Performs impact analyses and periodic reviews
